# Tauric Research

> A research organization (https://tauric.ai) that produces work at the
> intersection of LLM agents and finance. Authors of the
> [[TradingAgents Framework]].

## What it is

A research group with academic affiliations at **UCLA** and **MIT**
(Massachusetts Institute of Technology). Listed authors of the
[[2026-05-24 tauric-tradingagents-paper|TradingAgents paper]]:

- **Yijia Xiao** (UCLA, Tauric)
- **Edward Sun** (UCLA, Tauric)
- **Di Luo** (UCLA, MIT)
- **Wei Wang** (UCLA, Tauric)

GitHub org: https://github.com/TauricResearch

## Notable output

- [[TradingAgents Framework]] — open-source multi-agent stock-trading
  system. arXiv:2412.20138, latest revision v7 (3 Jun 2025).

## When to engage with their work

- For **reference architectures** in multi-agent LLM finance. ✅
- For **communication-protocol design** (structured-doc + NL-debate hybrid). ✅
- For **production trading signals**. ❌ — their published evaluation is
  too narrow to support live deployment. See [[Backtest Validity]].
