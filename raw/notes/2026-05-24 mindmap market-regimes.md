## My read on what's interesting

- The **three-pillar structure** is the load-bearing idea: macro,
  structural/regulatory, micro. Most "regime detection" frameworks collapse
  into pillar 3 only (technicals/factor rotation). This explicitly says
  regulation matters as much as price action.
- The 1991 liberalization and SEBI/ASM examples mean this is built for
  Indian equity markets. The framework generalizes but the specific
  trigger examples are India-flavored.
- "Hindsight bias" called out by name in practical challenges is honest —
  most regime frameworks read clean ex-post and are useless ex-ante.
